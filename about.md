I am a civil and industrial engineer from Guatemala, currently pursuing a doctoral degree in structural engineering at Hokkaido University in Japan. My interests center on structural and earthquake engineering, experimental testing, and understanding how structures respond to strong ground motions.

My professional experience includes civil and structural engineering projects involving retaining structures, foundations, building structures, structural analysis and modeling, and the preparation of calculation reports. I value careful technical work, collaboration, and learning from each project.

## Academic interests

My current research involves steel structures and experimental investigation of structural response. I am interested in connecting laboratory observations, data analysis, and practical engineering lessons.
