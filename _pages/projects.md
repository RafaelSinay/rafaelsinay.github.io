My engineering portfolio brings together selected projects from my professional experience in civil and structural engineering.

For each project, I plan to document the context, my responsibilities, the tools or standards involved when relevant, selected photographs or drawings that I am permitted to share, and the main lessons I learned.

### What I will include

- **Project overview:** type of project, location when appropriate, and general scope.
- **My contribution:** the tasks I carried out and my level of responsibility.
- **Technical work:** analysis, design, modeling, drawings, or reports, as applicable.
- **Lessons learned:** skills developed and engineering insights.
- **Media:** approved photographs, diagrams, or other materials that can be shared publicly.

*Confidential client information, private drawings, and restricted project details will not be published.*
