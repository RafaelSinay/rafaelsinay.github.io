# Rafael Sinay Oliva

**🏗️ Structural Engineering · Researcher**

🇬🇹 Guatemala · 📍 🇯🇵 Japan

I am a civil and industrial engineer from Guatemala, currently pursuing doctoral research in structural engineering at Hokkaido University in Japan. My interests include structural behavior, earthquake engineering, experimental testing, and making buildings safer and more resilient.

This website is my professional portfolio and personal archive: a place to share engineering projects, research progress, academic activities, and the things I enjoy beyond work.
