Since I graduated as a Civil Industrial Engineer from Universidad del Valle de Guatemala, I had a clear goal: **to continue my master’s studies through a scholarship**.

Thanks to the support of the **Japan Embassy in Guatemala**, that dream became a reality. I am incredibly grateful to share that since **April 2024 I have been awarded the MEXT Scholarship**, a fully-funded opportunity that allows me to pursue advanced studies in Japan.  

You can see more about this moment on my Facebook here:
